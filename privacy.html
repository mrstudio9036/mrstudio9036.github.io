Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Summary

Snarrow is a free puzzle game by MR Studio. You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, hints, undos, settings and daily gift streak) is saved only on your device. We do not upload it to any server. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Ads

Snarrow shows video ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This data goes to Google, not to MR Studio. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Updates and ratings

The game uses Google Play services to check for new versions and to let you rate the game. These features are handled by Google Play.

Children

Snarrow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and on our privacy page, with a new date.
