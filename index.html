Snarrow

ARROW PUZZLE GAME

Clear the board. One arrow at a time.

Snarrow is a relaxing logic puzzle. Every arrow slides out like a snake along its own path – but only if the way ahead of its tip is clear. Find the right order, keep your three hearts, and clear 200 hand-tuned levels from Easy to Hard. Pinch to zoom on big boards, hold an arrow to preview its path, and come back every day for a new Daily Challenge.
